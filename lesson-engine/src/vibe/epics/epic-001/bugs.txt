## Bug-002 - Increase Animated Background Movement

Status: complete
Notes: Added three smooth CSS gradient layers with distinct 9s, 13s and 15s multidirectional paths, restored the #4b6982 grey-blue token, and retained the reduced-motion fallback.

The current animated gradient background is far too subtle. The movement is barely noticeable, making the background appear almost static.

Requirements:
- Make the gradient animation significantly more noticeable.
- The colours should visibly move and flow around the screen.
- Rather than simply shifting from one side to another, the gradient should move organically in multiple directions.
- Introduce more variation in the movement, including horizontal, vertical and diagonal motion.
- Use multiple animated gradient layers with different animation speeds and movement patterns to create a fluid, random-looking effect.
- Avoid obvious repetitive movement or sudden changes in direction.
- Increase the range of movement so that different colours visibly occupy different areas of the screen over time.
- The animation should feel dynamic and alive while remaining smooth and relaxing.
- Aim for visible movement within approximately 2–3 seconds of watching the background.
- Consider using larger gradient layers, animated background positions or moving radial gradients to achieve this.
- Keep the existing slate grey-blue and deep teal colour palette established in Bug-001.
- Do not reintroduce purple tones.
- Keep all animation styling centralised and reusable throughout the application.
- Prefer CSS animations over JavaScript.
- Ensure the animation performs smoothly on mobile devices, desktops and televisions.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion by disabling or significantly reducing movement when enabled.

Acceptance Criteria:
- The background is clearly animated when viewed for a few seconds.
- Colours visibly flow across different areas of the screen.
- Movement occurs in multiple directions rather than following a simple linear pattern.
- The animation appears organic rather than obviously repetitive.
- Transitions remain smooth without abrupt jumps or flickering.
- The slate grey-blue and deep teal palette is preserved.
- The animation does not noticeably impact interface responsiveness.
- The updated animation applies consistently across all application pages.
